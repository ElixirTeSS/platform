## About TeSSHub4EOSC

TeSSHub4EOSC is a pilot hub for community-managed training catalogues for EOSC Nodes.

[Visit TeSSHub4EOSC](https://tesshub4eosc.eu/).

It supports spaces for EOSC Nodes, clusters, and communities. See the [project page]({{ "/projects/tesshub4eosc" | relative_url }}) for further details.

- Spaces can be created for EOSC Nodes, clusters and communities, such as [PaNOSC Node](https://panosc.tesshub4eosc.hzdr.de/) and [CERN Node](https://cern.tesshub4eosc.hzdr.de/).
- See a [video demonstration of managing spaces and sources in TeSSHub4EOSC](https://youtu.be/UFLGVlTniJs).
