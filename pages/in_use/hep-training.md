## About HEP Training

HEP Training provides training and events for the High Energy Physics community.

[Visit HEP Training](http://heptraining.cern.ch/).

HEP Training includes private spaces, allowing specific groups of users to view materials.For more information, visit [Introduction to Groups](https://elixirtess.github.io/docs/groups/intro-groups/) in the TeSS Platform documentaiton.
